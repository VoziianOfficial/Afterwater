Your privacy.

A clear view of how enquiry information is handled.

What you shareHow information is usedService providersRetention and requestsMessages and repliesDelivery and technical dataYour requestsSharing and your provider

What you share

An enquiry includes your name, email, selected service, affected space and message. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Your details are used to review and respond to your enquiry. Any proposed onward sharing with an independent provider should be explained before it occurs. Enquiry information is not sold.

Service providers

Website hosting and email providers may process information needed to run this website and deliver messages. Your chosen restoration provider has its own privacy practices.

Retention and requests

Information should be retained only as needed to handle the enquiry and applicable obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion.

Messages and replies

The enquiry form requests your name, email address, selected service, affected space and message. Required fields help us understand the request and reply to the address you provide.

The message is intended for property and service context. Please avoid sending identification documents, financial details or private information about other people.

Delivery and technical data

The form sends its contents to the website’s configured email recipient. Hosting and email systems may process technical information needed to receive, deliver and protect those messages.

If delivery fails, the website displays an error. You may contact the email address shown on this page instead. A successful on-screen response confirms server acceptance, not a provider booking.

Your requests

You can contact us to ask about an enquiry, correct information you supplied or request its deletion. Include enough context to identify the relevant conversation.

For access or deletion requests, we may need to confirm that the request relates to your information. Applicable requirements and any necessary record retention are considered when responding.

Sharing and your provider

Sending an enquiry does not authorise every independent provider to contact you. Any proposed onward sharing should be explained in the context of your request.

If you contact or appoint a provider directly, its own privacy notice governs information you share with that business. Ask the provider about its handling of assessment records and photographs.
