Our role

Afterwater is an independent discovery platform, not a restoration contractor. We help you explore water damage services and prepare an enquiry for independent providers. Availability, scope, pricing and all work are agreed directly with your chosen provider.

Your chosen provider

Confirm suitability, credentials, availability, scope and terms directly with the provider. The platform does not carry out work, set provider prices or guarantee outcomes.

Information and imagery

Content is general information, not an assessment of your property. Generated imagery and comparisons illustrate possible situations, not completed client projects or specific members of a provider network.

Enquiries and agreements

Sending an enquiry does not create a restoration contract. Review a written quotation, the scope, exclusions and payment terms directly with your provider.

Scope and suitability

Website content helps you prepare questions about water extraction, drying and repairs. It does not determine the condition of a property or prescribe a technical treatment.

A provider’s assessment is needed to understand the specific circumstances, materials, access and scope. Discuss any uncertainty before agreeing to work.

Quotes and agreements

Request a written quotation that explains the proposed work, assessment charges, exclusions and any further costs that may arise. Prices and availability are set by independent providers.

An enquiry through this website does not reserve an appointment or create a restoration contract. Any booking, payment, cancellation and warranty arrangements are agreed directly with the chosen provider.

Responsible use

Provide an accurate description to the best of your knowledge and use an email address at which you can receive replies. Do not submit abusive messages, unsolicited advertising or information you have no right to share.

Automated misuse or submissions that interfere with the website may be rejected. Form validation and submission limits help maintain the enquiry service.

Availability and changes

The website may be updated as services, content and contact details change. Temporary interruptions can occur, including when email delivery or hosting is unavailable.

If a form submission fails, follow the displayed message or contact the published email address. Do not treat an unconfirmed submission as an accepted booking.
