Essential storage

This website stores your cookie choice in local storage so that the notice does not appear on every page. This is browser storage, not a tracking cookie.

No tracking installed

No analytics or advertising trackers are installed. Choosing Accept does not enable additional tracking.

Change your preference

Use Cookie settings in the footer to reopen the notice. You can also clear site data using your browser settings.

External services

If you leave this website, the destination service has its own privacy and storage practices.

Preference storage

The key afterwater-consent stores your choice in this browser’s local storage. Its value records whether you selected Accept or Essential only.

The preference remains until you clear site data or change it through Cookie settings in the footer. It does not create an account or follow you to another device.

Form session

On a PHP-enabled host, the contact handler can start a server session to limit rapid repeated submissions. This session stores the time of the most recent submission attempt.

The browser may receive a session cookie for that purpose. Its duration and attributes depend on the hosting configuration; it is separate from the saved cookie preference.

Search and interactions

Website search filters the built-in list of pages and sections in your browser. Entering a search query does not submit the enquiry form.

Image sliders, tabs and comparison controls work within the page. No advertising or analytics tracker is enabled by the Accept button.

Browser controls

You can review or clear local storage and cookies in your browser’s site settings. Clearing the saved preference makes the notice appear again.

Blocking storage may prevent your choice from being remembered. The pages remain available, while the behaviour of form sessions depends on the host and browser settings.
