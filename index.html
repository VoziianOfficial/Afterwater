Make the conversation count.

Useful questions for your independent provider.

What does the assessment cover?

Ask which rooms and materials will be reviewed, what information will be recorded and whether there is an assessment charge. Clarify how the findings will help you decide what happens next.

Before an assessment

How will the findings be explained?

Request a clear account of the visible changes and readings, including what remains uncertain and what needs further investigation. Ask for a record you can refer to when comparing the proposed next steps.

When reviewing findings

What can be retained?

Discuss sound materials, repair options and the effect of matching new finishes to the rest of your home. Request a clear explanation of preparation, samples and any limitations before agreeing the finish.

Before agreeing repairs

What belongs in the written scope?

Ask for equipment, visits, preparation and finishing to be itemised. Confirm exclusions, access arrangements and how any changes will be agreed before work begins.

Before making a decision
