Can the original materials be retained?

That depends on their assessed condition. Discuss repair and replacement options with your chosen provider.

When should restoration begin?

Ask the provider how the source and moisture conditions have been addressed before repairs or finishes are applied.

Can I match the existing finish?

Share details of the original materials and ask about samples, matching limitations and how adjacent areas will be treated.

Who agrees the price and scope?

You and your chosen independent provider. Confirm assessment charges, exclusions and the written scope before proceeding.
